1. What ClipWorld Publisher is

ClipWorld Publisher is a private tool operated by the owner of the "Official GTA VI ClipWorld" social media accounts. It uploads short vertical gaming highlight clips, prepared by the owner, to the owner's own accounts on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube.

2. Who can use it

The tool is not offered to the public. Only the account owner signs in and authorises it. No other person can create an account or publish through it.

3. Content

  • All clips are published to the owner's accounts only, and the owner is responsible for them.
  • Clips credit the original streamer by name and link to their channel.
  • The owner follows the community guidelines and terms of each platform (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube) and removes content when asked by a rights holder.

4. Platform terms

Publishing on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube is also governed by the terms of those platforms. Nothing in these terms changes them.

5. Changes and contact

These terms may be updated; the date above shows the latest version. For questions or removal requests, send a direct message to @officialgtaviclipsworld on Instagram.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

What data the tool uses

  • The access tokens that the account owner grants when signing in to TikTok, Instagram and YouTube.
  • Basic profile information of the owner's own accounts (account name and ID), used only to confirm which account receives the upload.
  • The video files and captions that the owner publishes.

What the tool does not do

  • It does not collect data about other TikTok, Instagram or YouTube users or viewers.
  • It does not read messages, comments, followers or contact lists.
  • It does not sell, rent or share any data with third parties.
  • It does not use cookies or tracking on this page.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are used only to publish the owner's videos. They are never published or sent anywhere other than the official TikTok, Meta and Google APIs.

Deleting data

The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time from the settings of each platform (TikTok: Manage app permissions; Instagram/Meta: Apps and websites; Google: Third-party access). Revoking access and deleting the local token files removes all data the tool holds.
